# CUMCM 2026 compliance baseline

This is a convenience checklist derived from the organizing committee's `全国大学生数学建模竞赛论文格式规范（2026 年修订稿）`. Re-check the official website and local official notices before submission if they have changed. Record the verified source and hash in `FORMAT_LOCK.json`; see `cumcm-template-lock.md`.

## Paper

- A4 paper; margins at least 2.5 cm.
- Printed paper order: pledge page, number page, summary page, then body.
- Summary page includes title and keywords and is no more than one page; page numbering starts at 1 on this page.
- The body starts on the next page, has no table of contents, and is no more than 30 pages; appendices are not page-limited.
- The appendix lists supporting files and contains all complete runnable source code.
- The electronic paper is one Word or PDF file, PDF recommended, no more than 20 MB; it excludes pledge and number pages and starts with the summary page.
- Supporting files are a ZIP/RAR archive no more than 20 MB and are listed in the appendix.
- Remove names, schools, instructor information, and other identity clues from the paper and supporting files.

## Typography boundary

- The 2026 national specification does not impose a uniform font size, font family, line spacing, or color; regional rules may add requirements.
- Therefore a black heading system, exact Chinese fonts, heading numbering, and spacing are a locked team style unless a verified official/regional template requires them.
- Do not describe a course template, prompt pack, or past winning paper as the official template.

## AI use

- AI may be used only as an auxiliary tool; the team leads the core modeling and analysis.
- Manually review and verify AI output before adoption.
- Put an AI-use declaration before the references.
- Submit the official AI-use detail file recording tool/version, purpose and phase, prompt/process, adopted content, manual changes, and verification.
- Obtain the exact tool/model/version from the client or service; never infer it.

## Conduct

Work independently and comply with current restrictions on discussing or browsing solution-related content for the live problem on public or group platforms. Official rules take precedence over training notes, previous papers, prompt packs, and this checklist.
