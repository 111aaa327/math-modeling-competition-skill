# CUMCM 2026 compliance baseline

This is a convenience checklist derived from the organizing committee's `全国大学生数学建模竞赛论文格式规范（2026 年修订稿）`. Verify the binary source against `cumcm-2026-format-fingerprint.md`, then record its path and hash in `FORMAT_LOCK.json`. A later official or regional notice still takes precedence.

## Paper

- White A4 paper; margins at least 2.5 cm on all four sides; left binding for the printed copy.
- Printed paper order: pledge page, number page, summary page, then body.
- The third printed page is the summary page. It includes the title, abstract, and keywords, needs no English translation, and is no more than one page; continuous Arabic page numbering starts at 1 in the centered footer on this page.
- The body starts on the next page, has no table of contents, and is no more than 30 pages; appendices are not page-limited.
- The appendix is printed and bound with the body. It lists supporting files and contains all complete runnable source code, including relevant Excel or SPSS interactive commands; if no program was used, say so explicitly.
- The electronic paper is one standalone Word or PDF file, PDF recommended, no more than 20 MB. Do not compress it. It excludes pledge and number pages and starts with the summary page.
- The electronic and printed papers must have identical content and formatting, including appendices.
- Supporting materials are a single ZIP/RAR archive no more than 20 MB. Include all necessary evidence for the model, results, and conclusions: complete runnable code, self-collected data other than problem-supplied raw data, and plots or files for large intermediate results. List every file in the paper appendix. If none are needed, state that fact in the appendix.
- Do not place the pledge or number page in the electronic paper or supporting archive.
- Remove names, schools, instructor information, and other identity clues from the paper and supporting files.

## Typography boundary

- The 2026 national specification does not impose a uniform font size, font family, line spacing, or color; regional rules may add requirements.
- Therefore a black heading system, exact Chinese fonts, heading numbering, and spacing are a locked team style unless a verified official/regional template requires them.
- Do not describe a course template, prompt pack, or past winning paper as the official template.

## Source boundary

- Lock only the format and submission clauses verified directly in the fingerprinted source.
- Do not enforce declarations, detail files, or other requirements taken from unverified external links. Add a new rule only after verifying it against a current authoritative notice.

## Conduct

Work independently and comply with current restrictions on discussing or browsing solution-related content for the live problem on public or group platforms. Official rules take precedence over training notes, previous papers, prompt packs, and this checklist.
